The drafts provide a useful starting point, but require **revision** before use as firm-policy templates. The most important corrections are: separate full IFRS from IFRS for SMEs; add genuine completeness procedures; correct investment measurement and impairment branches; strengthen disclosure evidence; and expressly test closing journals and adjustments rather than merely record them. ISA 330.18, .20 and .24 require substantive coverage of material balances, transactions and disclosures, reconciliation to accounting records, examination of material closing adjustments, and evaluation of presentation. [mia.org](https://mia.org.my/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/MIA_ISA_330.pdf)

Some submitted sentences and assertion lists are truncated. I have reviewed the identifiable procedure on every numbered line without treating missing text as confirmed wording.

## Review conventions and framework boundaries

- The revised lists retain your format: `Procedure (standard paragraphs). — type — [assertions]`.
- `[Conditional: …]` identifies an applicability condition. N/A requires a documented reason; it is not a substitute for assessing the condition.
- IFRS paragraphs prescribe accounting outcomes. ISAs normally require risk-responsive evidence, not a universal subject-specific checklist. The procedures below distinguish necessary accounting coverage from optional methods of obtaining that evidence. ISA 330.6–7, .18–21. [mia.org](https://mia.org.my/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/MIA_ISA_330.pdf)
- The IFAC Guide supports proportionate, risk-based audit planning, testing and documentation—particularly Volume 2, Chapters 16–18 and 21. Its fourth edition predates ISA 540 (Revised) and ISA 315 (Revised 2019), so those standards take precedence over older guidance. [ifac](https://www.ifac.org/knowledge-gateway/audit-assurance/publications/guide-using-international-standards-auditing-audits-small-and-medium-sized-entities)
- Select the reporting framework and applicable edition before enabling accounting branches. The IFRS for SMEs third edition applies for periods beginning on or after 1 January 2027, with early application permitted. IFRS 18 also applies from that date, with early application permitted; therefore IAS 1 references below apply where IFRS 18 has not been adopted. [ifrs](https://www.ifrs.org/content/dam/ifrs/publications/html-standards/english/2025/issued/html-ifrs-for-smes.html)
- None of the submitted lines is a genuine test of operating effectiveness. Do not relabel management approval, auditor review or documentation as `control`. Add or cross-reference actual control tests where reliance is planned or substantive procedures alone cannot provide sufficient evidence—ISA 330.8–10. [mia.org](https://mia.org.my/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/MIA_ISA_330.pdf)
- Your three-type taxonomy has no appropriate category for administrative documentation, overall evaluations or ISA 720 reading procedures. Where retained below, `test_of_detail` is a platform-category proxy, not a claim that ISA classifies the activity as a substantive test. Ideally, store a separate activity-purpose field. `fs_level` likewise denotes scope, not an ISA assertion.

Across all sections, test the completeness and accuracy of entity-produced information used as evidence, select items responsively to risk, and document exceptions and conclusions. These requirements should be global policy rather than repeated in every template—ISA 500.9–10; ISA 330.28–30. [mia.org](https://mia.org.my/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/MIA_ISA_330.pdf)

## 1. Intangible Assets & Goodwill

### Missing coverage and substantive corrections

1. Annual impairment testing also covers intangibles not yet available for use—not just goodwill and indefinite-life assets. A “no indicators” conclusion cannot replace a mandatory annual test: IAS 36.9–10, .90. [bdo](https://www.bdo.global/getmedia/3ef39b97-e129-4275-a291-2208290823ac/IFRS-AS-IP-IAS-36-2024-25.pdf)
2. Amortisation needs an available-for-use date, residual-value assessment, contractual-life limits and annual reassessment: IAS 38.94, .97, .100–104, .109. [ifrs](https://www.ifrs.org/content/dam/ifrs/publications/html-standards/english/2026/issued/ias38.html)
3. Goodwill is not simply consideration minus identifiable net assets. The calculation includes applicable non-controlling interests and previously held interests; asset acquisitions must be distinguished from business combinations: IFRS 3.2(b), .18–19, .32, .34–36.
4. Impairment coverage should include model/data integrity, management bias, CGU consistency, allocation of losses, permitted reversals and the prohibition on goodwill reversals: ISA 540.18, .22–27, .32–36; IAS 36.75, .104–105, .110–125. Sensitivity analysis is useful where uncertainty warrants it, not an unconditional requirement for every estimate. [irba.co](https://www.irba.co.za/upload/ISA-540-Revised-and-Conforming-Amendments.pdf)
5. Full-IFRS rules cannot be applied unchanged to SMEs. IFRS for SMEs expenses internally generated intangible expenditure, including research and development, and amortises intangibles and goodwill: 18.14–15, .19–24; 19.23; impairment follows Section 27. [ifrs](https://www.ifrs.org/content/dam/ifrs/publications/html-standards/english/2025/issued/html-ifrs-for-smes.html)
6. Add source-to-register completeness work and, where applicable, revaluation-model testing. Schedule-to-GL agreement alone cannot establish that all eligible assets were recorded: ISA 330.A45; IAS 38.72–78. [mia.org](https://mia.org.my/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/MIA_ISA_330.pdf)

2. Equity accounting needs acquisition-date basis differences, distributions, unrealised-profit elimination, loss-recognition limits and impairment of the net investment: IAS 28.10, .28, .32–43. Long-term interests not equity-accounted also require IFRS 9 consideration: IAS 28.14A. [ifrs](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-28-investments-in-associates-and-joint-ventures/)

### Missing coverage and substantive corrections

1. Add examination of material closing journals and adjustments, including entries outside the GL. Recording proposed adjustments is not the test required by ISA 330.20(b). Cross-reference fraud-focused journal testing under ISA 240.32(a). [mia.org](https://mia.org.my/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/MIA_ISA_330.pdf)
2. Do not exclude accounts merely because each is below materiality. Consider aggregation, qualitative significance, material transaction classes and disclosures; all accounts must remain in the financial-statement population. ISA 330.18; ISA 450.11. [mia.org](https://mia.org.my/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/MIA_ISA_330.pdf)
3. A single TB account can support multiple audit areas and disclosures. Require a controlled primary mapping and appropriate secondary links—not “exactly one audit section” as an ISA rule.
4. Corrected/uncorrected describes correction status; clearly trivial describes whether accumulation is required. They are not three equivalent categories. Accumulate all non-clearly-trivial misstatements and distinguish factual, judgemental and projected misstatements where useful: ISA 450.5, A2–A6. [ibr-ire](https://www.ibr-ire.be/docs/default-source/fr/documents/reglementation-et-publications/normes-et-recommandations/isa/isa-english-version/isa-450_en.pdf?sfvrsn=ee26e4d9_1)

[ibr-ire](https://www.ibr-ire.be/docs/default-source/fr/documents/reglementation-et-publications/normes-et-recommandations/isa/isa-english-version/isa-450_en.pdf?sfvrsn=ee26e4d9_1)
6. Add evidence that agreed adjustments were actually reflected, version-difference testing and traceability to the authorised final FS. ISA 230 does not prescribe a particular lead-schedule format; it prescribes adequate documentation.

## Implementation notes for the firm policy

Keep these five templates as risk-responsive programmes, not unconditional checklists. Each selected procedure should record applicability, relevant risk/assertion, extent of testing, evidence, exceptions and conclusion. Cross-references are acceptable when they identify completed work that actually addresses the relevant objective; they should not merely point to another unfinished section. ISA 330.28–30 and the IFAC Guide’s documentation and responsive-planning guidance support this approach. [mia.org](https://mia.org.my/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/MIA_ISA_330.pdf)

Before release, version the templates by reporting framework and audit-standard effective date. Also separate administrative/evaluation activities from substantive and control procedures in the platform’s data model; forcing all activities into three audit-test categories otherwise creates misleading coverage reports.
