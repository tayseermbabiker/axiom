How Audexon keeps your data safe
  • Your client files stay in your own Google Drive or OneDrive — Audexon never stores them.
  • Audexon only stores what you enter: trial balance, procedures, findings, and review notes.
  • Only your firm's authorised team members can access your engagements.
  • Your data is protected with strong encryption at all times — whether it's being sent or stored.
  • You always own your data — Audexon simply helps you manage the audit work securely.
